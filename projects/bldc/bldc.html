BLDC Motor Controller (ESC)

Power Electronics
Arduino UNO C++ MOSFETs

Overview

An Electronic Speed Controller (ESC) for a 3-phase Brushless DC (BLDC) motor was designed, implemented, and tested using Arduino UNO, MOSFET-based power electronics, and PWM control techniques. The system utilizes 6-step commutation to provide reliable motor operation and adjustable speed control under different loading conditions.

My Role & Implementation

I participated in the practical implementation of the project, including hardware assembly, component integration, electrical wiring, system testing, troubleshooting, and performance validation. My responsibilities involved assembling the power stage, verifying electrical connections, conducting practical tests under different operating conditions, and assisting in hardware debugging and optimization to improve system reliability and stability.

Technical Highlights

  • 3-Phase MOSFET Inverter Design – Power stage for driving a 3-phase BLDC motor efficiently.
  • IR2110 High/Low-Side Gate Driver – Provides reliable switching and control of MOSFETs in the inverter stage.
  • PWM Speed Control – Adjustable motor speed control through PWM signal modulation.
  • 6-Step Commutation – Electronic commutation technique for BLDC motor operation.
  • Back-EMF and Hall-Sensor Compatible Architecture – Flexible motor control architecture supporting different feedback methods.
  • Voltage and Current Measurement Integration – Monitoring and testing capabilities for performance evaluation.
  • Hardware Debugging & Switching Optimization – Practical testing and optimization of switching behavior and controller stability.

Challenges & Problem Solving

One of the main challenges was achieving stable motor operation and reliable switching performance under varying operating conditions. Several rounds of testing and troubleshooting were performed to identify hardware-related issues, verify electrical connections, and improve overall system stability. Oscilloscope-based measurements and practical validation were used to analyze system behavior and optimize performance.

Results

The controller successfully operated a 3-phase BLDC motor with stable commutation and adjustable speed control under different load conditions. The project provided valuable hands-on experience in power electronics, motor drive systems, hardware testing, electrical troubleshooting, and practical implementation of embedded motor control solutions.
